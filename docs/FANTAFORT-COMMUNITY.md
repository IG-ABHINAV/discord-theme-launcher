## Accuracy gate for the introduction

The maintainer wants to explain the project's experimental availability and interest measurement. Before publishing any data claim, inspect the actual service and privacy implementation. The current [FantaFort README](https://github.com/PrimeBuild-pc/FantaFort) describes accounts, sessions, profile information and user-generated content. Therefore **do not publish a blanket statement that the whole service collects no personal data** based only on an intention to collect anonymous usage statistics.

Distinguish anonymous or aggregate usage analytics from account/service data, third-party hosting logs and Discord community participation. Link the actual [privacy notice](https://fantafort.com/privacy) and align the copy with implemented collection, retention and consent behavior. Explain alpha limitations and the independent, non-endorsed relationship with Epic Games. Do not describe an approval application as approved or pending without evidence.

The phrase "annual project" and the intended approval process also need clarification before being presented as public facts. Preserve those as open copy questions for this workstream rather than guessing their meaning.
